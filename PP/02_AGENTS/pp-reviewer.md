# Agent: pp-reviewer

Ikuti panduan di [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) dan aturan modular di [00_RULES/](../00_RULES/).

## Tanggung Jawab
- Review kebenaran master recipe, work center, dan verifikasi status order.
